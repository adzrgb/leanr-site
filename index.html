RETATRUTIDE

Triple GIP/GLP-1/GCG Receptor Agonist

Nutrition Guide While Taking Retatrutide

Eat Smart. Feel Better. Get Better Results.

Your nutrition plays a huge role in how well Retatrutide works. Choosing the right foods can help reduce side effects, boost results, and support long-term health.

Foods to Focus On

  • Lean Proteins: Chicken, turkey, fish, eggs, tofu, lean beef
  • Vegetables: High in fibre, vitamins and minerals
  • Healthy Fats: Avocado, nuts, seeds, olive oil
  • High Fibre Carbs: Sweet potatoes, oats, quinoa, brown rice
  • Hydrating Foods: Cucumber, watermelon, berries, citrus fruits

Foods to Limit or Avoid

  • Fried & Fast Foods: Can worsen nausea and slow digestion
  • Sugary Snacks & Desserts: Can cause energy crashes and cravings
  • Alcohol: Can increase nausea and dehydration
  • White Bread, Pasta & Rice: Can cause blood sugar spikes
  • Carbonated & Sugary Drinks: Can increase bloating

Protein Intake: Aim for 1.2–1.6g per kg of body weight daily

Water Intake: Aim for 2–3 litres per day

General Tips: Eat smaller, balanced meals. Listen to your body and don't overeat. Stay consistent and be patient.
